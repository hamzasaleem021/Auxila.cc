Unfinished things don't stay quiet. The mind replays them until they're done or set down. A to-do list doesn't help much, because it never says what each item needs. Open Loops does.

Open Loops

A calm place for unfinished things.

Get unfinished things out of your head. Give each one a kind (do, decide, ask, schedule or let go) and a single next step.

For people who start more than they finish.

Liner notes

Feeds got clever and reading got worse. The more an app picks for you, the less you chose. Morning Brief goes the other way. It's a brief you can finish, not a feed you scroll.

Product, design and build by Hamza Saleem. Made with AI.

AUX 002 · Version 26

AUX 002 · Reading · Version 26

Morning Brief

Your sources. Your signal. Every morning.

Choose up to twenty sources from 1,250 curated feeds, or paste your own. Open it and you get each source's five latest posts, in the order you set. Nothing ranks them, and nothing watches you read.

For readers who'd rather pick their own front page.

20sources, by design. Enough for a brief. Too few for a feed.
